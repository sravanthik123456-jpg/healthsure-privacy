HealthSure Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

HealthSure is designed to provide a simple health management experience. We respect your privacy and aim to protect your information.

2. Information Collection

Based on the currently inspected version of HealthSure, the app does not provide a functioning account registration or health-data submission feature. We have not identified code that intentionally collects personal or health information.

3. Device Permissions

The currently inspected Android app declares Internet permission. It does not declare camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions.

4. Information Sharing

We have not identified an implemented feature that shares personal or health information with third parties. This may change if new features or services are added.

5. Data Security

We take privacy seriously. No system can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid entering sensitive health information into features that are not yet fully implemented.

6. Children's Privacy

HealthSure is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through the currently inspected app features.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
